# SafeAgent Strategic Acquirer Program

Status: ACTIVE
Owner: Zender Gaming Technologies LLC
Purpose: create a professional strategic-acquisition lane for SafeAgent without confusing technical validation with acquisition interest.

## Objective

Create multiple credible strategic conversations with organizations for which SafeAgent could fill a missing execution-control layer around consequential AI-agent actions.

This program does **not** assume any target will acquire SafeAgent. It tracks fit, evidence, professional routes, objections, diligence readiness, and real acquisition signals.

## Acquisition thesis

SafeAgent is not another agent framework or generic AI security product. The acquisition thesis is narrower:

> AI platforms increasingly govern identity, prompts, models, workflows, and telemetry, but consequential external actions still need an execution-integrity layer that can claim before execution, prevent duplicate/replayed side effects, preserve unresolved outcomes, return durable receipts, and reconcile what actually happened.

A strategic buyer could use SafeAgent to extend an existing AI gateway, workflow/orchestration, observability, agent-security, or agentic-commerce platform from "observe/govern the agent" into "control and prove the consequence."

## What counts as acquisition signal

Do not count:
- stars, installs, downloads, mentions, compliments, or generic interest;
- a request to send the GitHub link;
- product-adjacent acquisition activity by itself.

Count progressively:
1. **STRATEGIC FIT CONFIRMED** — a product/corp-dev owner confirms the gap is relevant.
2. **DILIGENCE REQUESTED** — architecture, security, IP, customer/evaluator, or financial information requested.
3. **STRATEGIC DISCUSSION** — partnership, licensing, investment, or acquisition is explicitly discussed.
4. **PROCESS OPENED** — NDA, term-sheet process, data-room access, formal corp-dev diligence, or written indication of interest.
5. **TRANSACTION** — signed acquisition/license/investment agreement.

## Account workflow

For each target:

1. Confirm current product thesis and recent M&A/partnership behavior.
2. Map SafeAgent to one specific missing capability; no generic "AI safety" pitch.
3. Identify two professional routes:
   - primary: corporate development / product GM / strategic partnerships;
   - secondary: official technology alliance, partner, vendor, or startup program.
4. Prepare one 1-page strategic brief:
   - their current product;
   - their documented gap/risk;
   - SafeAgent capability;
   - integration surface;
   - build-vs-buy rationale;
   - evidence package;
   - bounded evaluation.
5. Do not contact until Anthony approves the exact target, route, and message.
6. Record response as NO RESPONSE, PRODUCT INTEREST, STRATEGIC FIT CONFIRMED, DILIGENCE REQUESTED, STRATEGIC DISCUSSION, or PROCESS OPENED.
