## SafeAgent thesis

ServiceNow already controls roles, policies, identities, and workflow orchestration. SafeAgent's potential gap is narrower: the boundary where an autonomous workflow causes an external irreversible effect and confirmation is lost.

SafeAgent could provide:
- consequence claim before dispatch;
- one-use execution permit;
- explicit PENDING/SETTLED state;
- duplicate/replay blocking;
- provider readback/reconciliation;
- durable receipt tied to the logical action.
